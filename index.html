01 — About

A data function, built from nothing, during hypergrowth.

When I joined Next Ventures in 2020 there was no warehouse, no pipelines, and no reporting layer. Numbers came from whoever could export them. I was the data function.

In the years since, FundedNext has scaled to more than $372 million distributed to traders across 450,000+ accounts in 170+ countries. The data function grew with it — from one analyst to fifteen people across two management layers, with analysts embedded directly in the CFDs, Futures, and Broker business lines.

Along the way my job moved from building dashboards to building the team, standards, and platform that let other people do it well. I also took on product ownership of CFDs, so I am accountable for the decisions the data informs — not just the data.

04 — Principles

How I work

  1. Metric definitions are a governance problem, not a technical one.

    The hardest thing I have enforced is not a pipeline or a schema. It is getting three business lines to agree on what a single contested metric means. The SQL was the easy half.

  2. Embedded beats centralised, once you can afford it.

    A central queue optimises for analyst utilisation. Embedding optimises for decisions actually changing. Those are different goals, and the second is the one that matters.

  3. Adoption is the deliverable.

    I have shipped work that was technically sound and went unused. A dashboard nobody opens is not a partial success; it is a signal the problem was never properly understood.

  4. Demand will always exceed capacity.

    The answer is not heroics. It is a legible intake process, an honest backlog, and the willingness to tell stakeholders what their request costs and what it displaces.
